Rice seed varieties: derived teaching subset of Vu et al. (2020), "RGB and VIS/NIR Hyperspectral
Imaging Data for 90 Rice Seed Varieties", https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3241923 (paper
https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.2969847), licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: 10 varieties x 24 kernels; RGB kernel crop
padded to square and resized to 32x32; three 3x3-mean calibrated pixel spectra (256 bands, 383-1006 nm);
stratified 19 train / 5 test kernels per variety. Target: variety (10 classes, classification).
Limitation: each variety comes from a single imaging bundle (variety confounded with acquisition).
